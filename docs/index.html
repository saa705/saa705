Career Aspirations

I am particularly interested in roles within public sector organisations and critical infrastructure environments, where cybersecurity is not just about business continuity but national safety. I aim to continuously build both technical and analytical skills, supported by certifications like CompTIA Security+ and hands-on experience through labs and simulations.
