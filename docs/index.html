ISYS10401 – Professional Development for Industry

Career Aspirations

My primary career aspiration is to become a Cybersecurity Analyst, a role that plays a critical part in protecting digital systems, networks, and data from cyber threats. This career appeals to me due to the increasing importance of information security in today's digital world and the opportunity to make a meaningful impact by identifying vulnerabilities, analysing threats, and implementing protective measures. Through industry research, I have learned that successful cybersecurity professionals must have a strong understanding of network protocols, risk management, threat analysis, and ethical hacking practices. Many employers also look for familiarity with tools such as Wireshark, Kali Linux, and security frameworks like ISO 27001 and NIST. I am particularly interested in roles within public sector organisations and critical infrastructure environments, where cybersecurity is not just about business continuity but national safety. I aim to continuously build both technical and analytical skills, supported by certifications like CompTIA Security+ and hands-on experience through labs and simulations.

Analysis of My Skills

In relation to my goal of becoming a Cybersecurity Analyst, I have developed a foundational understanding of computer networks, operating systems, and threat identification through modules such as Network Fundamentals and Introduction to Cybersecurity. I have gained hands-on experience using network analysis tools like Wireshark and practicing password security and basic encryption techniques during lab sessions. In group projects, I demonstrated good analytical thinking and team collaboration, both key skills in cybersecurity investigations. I have also improved my technical writing by documenting findings clearly and professionally in lab reports. However, I recognise the need to improve my familiarity with penetration testing tools like Metasploit and to gain deeper knowledge of frameworks such as NIST and ISO 27001. Additionally, developing scripting skills in Python and Bash will help automate tasks such as log analysis and vulnerability scanning. These gaps form the basis of my short-term and long-term professional development plans.

Knowledge & Skill Development (CPD)

To address the gaps in my cybersecurity skills, I have planned and undertaken a range of CPD (Continuing Professional Development) activities. I completed an online course titled "Introduction to Ethical Hacking" via FutureLearn, which provided practical exposure to penetration testing fundamentals. I also participated in a university-led cybersecurity awareness workshop, which introduced real-world case studies and response strategies. Additionally, I spent several hours working on TryHackMe labs to reinforce hands-on skills. These activities helped me understand vulnerabilities, strengthen my defensive thinking, and apply theoretical concepts in practical environments. Reflecting on these experiences, I recognise that active learning is essential for cybersecurity careers due to the fast-changing nature of threats. In the short term, I plan to continue using TryHackMe and pursue CompTIA Security+ certification. In the long term, I aim to work toward a CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker) qualification while gaining industry experience.

Reflection on Professional Conduct

Throughout this module, I have developed a stronger understanding of professionalism in the IT industry. By engaging with the BCS Code of Conduct, I now appreciate the importance of integrity, confidentiality, and public interest when working with sensitive systems and data. I have attended all lectures and workshops punctually, contributed to group discussions, and maintained respectful communication with peers and staff. These actions reflect professional behaviours expected in the workplace. However, I also noticed areas where I can improve — particularly in time management and confidence when presenting ideas during seminars. I intend to work on these areas by setting clearer deadlines for self-learning and seeking feedback from tutors after delivering presentations. Upholding professional standards will be essential in my future career as a Cybersecurity Analyst, especially when dealing with incident reports, client data, or coordinating with cross-functional teams.
